RESERVSYS OFFICE SPACE RULES

1. Office Opening Hours
The Warsaw and Poznan offices are open for employees from Monday to Friday, between 07:00 AM and 08:00 PM. Staying in the office outside these hours requires prior notification to the administration.

2. Hot-desking Rules
- Every employee must reserve a desk through the ReservSys system before coming to the office.
- Desks in the "Open Space" zone are intended for regular daily work. Moderate-level conversations are allowed.
- Desks in the "Quiet Zone" are for focused work. Absolute silence is required in this zone, and phone calls are strictly prohibited.
- "Private Room" booths can be reserved for a maximum of 3 hours per day for important online meetings or confidential calls.
- If you know you will not come to the office, you must cancel your reservation at least one hour before it starts.

3. Office Equipment and Cleanliness
- Upon finishing work, employees must leave the desk empty and clean (Clean Desk Policy).
- Leaving cups, plates, and personal belongings on desks after leaving the office is prohibited. Items left after 08:00 PM will be removed by the cleaning service.

4. Kitchen and Social Area
- Refrigerators are cleaned every Friday after 04:00 PM. Unsigned food items will be thrown away.
- Please empty the coffee machine grounds when the prompt appears on the screen. Do not leave this task for the next person!
